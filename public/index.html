Security

Your ERP runs your business.
We treat access to it that way.

BespoxAI has to reach your system to be useful. This is exactly how that access works — and what keeps it in your hands.

🚪

No open ports

The BespoxAI Agent connects out to us through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel. No inbound firewall rules, no VPN, and nothing on your network exposed to the internet.

🖥️

An agent that stays in its lane

The agent runs on your server under the Windows account you choose — never as SYSTEM. It only answers requests arriving through its own tunnel, and each one must carry a key unique to your environment.

👁️

Answers only read

The CFO Assistant reads your data to answer a question. It never posts, edits or deletes anything, and it can only see what the BC user you set up is permitted to see.

✅

Nothing goes live without you

Development goes to your test environment first. Production follows only after your administrator signs off testing and approves go-live — and only the exact build you tested can be deployed.

🔑

Remote support by consent

Remote desktop access for our developers stays off until your administrator turns it on, and can be turned off at any time. Every connection needs a one-time code and a second factor, is limited to the BespoxAI developers working on your account, and is logged.

🛡️

A portal built for finance

Only your administrators can approve quotes, make payments, change connection settings or approve go-live. Repeated failed sign-ins are blocked, and idle sessions sign out automatically.

Have a security questionnaire to get through? Send it to Admin@bespoxai.com and a developer will answer it. More detail in the FAQ.
