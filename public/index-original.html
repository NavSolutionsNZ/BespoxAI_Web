What's your main challenge?

You need an answer—now, not in weeks

You have a modification idea. You contact your existing partner. They book you in—three weeks from now. The consultation happens. Discovery, questions, drafting a Functional Requirements Document. Days or weeks. Then the estimate. Weeks more. Thousands of dollars in hidden consulting costs before you even know the price.

With BespoxAI, you start generating that document today. Minutes. Our AI, grounded in an index of your NAV or BC version and its objects, walks you through the requirements in real time. You refine it yourself. Then you request a senior developer review. They ask clarifying questions, you finalize the spec, and you get a transparent quote. Scope locked. No surprises.

Your partner is great—just overbooked

We're not here to replace your partner. We exist because they're busy—and they should be. When you have a modification or enhancement that needs exploring, your partner is often focused on larger deliverables, bigger customers, and pipeline priorities. That's not a failure of theirs; it's reality. But your idea deserves exploration without a three-month lag.

BespoxAI handles the rapid discovery and specification phase. You work with our AI and senior developers to define requirements clearly, get a transparent estimate, and prove concept validity—all in days, not months. Then you can confidently take that specification to your partner, negotiate scope, or decide whether to build internally.

Hidden costs and unclear scope—not anymore

A simple modification request to your partner becomes a multi-step cost center: consultant time for discovery, developer time for estimation, project management overhead—all baked into a final quote you can't unpack. You pay for the entire process, whether the idea is worth pursuing or not.

With BespoxAI, you see exactly what you're building before you commit. No hidden consulting fees. Scope is locked. You decide with full visibility.
