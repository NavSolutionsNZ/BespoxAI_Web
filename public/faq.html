Getting started
Which versions of NAV and Business Central do you support?

Dynamics NAV 2009 and later — Classic and RoleTailored clients — along with Business Central on-premises, ISV Embed deployments, and Business Central SaaS. If your system runs C/AL or AL, we speak its language.

Do we need to upgrade or migrate first?

No. That's the point. BespoxAI works with the system you own today, as it is today — customisations, vertical solutions and all. We will never make you migrate as the price of getting modern tooling.

Do we need an active enhancement plan or a Microsoft partner?

No. Whether your enhancement plan lapsed years ago or you still have an active partner relationship, BespoxAI works alongside whatever support arrangement you have — including none. If you do have a partner, you can take everything we produce straight to them.

How long until we see value?

Days, not months. Onboarding is an automated environment ingest, not an implementation project — most environments are live within days of connecting.

Your system and your customisations
We're heavily customised. Does that break this?

The opposite — it's why we exist. BespoxAI indexes your objects: customisations, vertical solutions and bespoke ranges included. Answers reflect your system as it's actually built, not a textbook copy of standard NAV.

Does it work with vertical and ISV solutions?

Yes. The object index covers your full licensed object range, not just the base application — dealer management, manufacturing add-ons and other verticals are treated as first-class parts of your system, because to your business, they are.

Does BespoxAI replace our NAV developer?

No — it makes whoever supports your system faster and less dependent on tribal knowledge. With C/AL developers redirecting to AL and Business Central work faster than they're being replaced, think of it as insurance for the knowledge locked inside your system, not a redundancy plan for the people who hold it.

Security and data
How does BespoxAI connect to our system?

Through an outbound-only encrypted tunnel (Cloudflare Tunnel). A lightweight connector on your network dials out — no inbound firewall rules, no open ports, no part of your ERP exposed to the internet. Your perimeter stays exactly as closed as it is today.

What data does BespoxAI access?

Two things, for two purposes. First, your object metadata — table, codeunit and report definitions — is indexed so BespoxAI understands how your system is built, customisations included.

Second, when you ask a question that needs live figures, a scoped query runs against your environment and the result comes back through the tunnel to answer it. Query results are used to answer your question and are not stored by BespoxAI.

Can BespoxAI change anything in our system?

No — and not just as policy. The connector authenticates with read-only credentials that you provision and control. Writing to your database isn't something we choose not to do; it's something the access you grant makes impossible. You can audit, scope, or revoke that access at any time using the same SQL and NAV security tools you use today.

Who can see financial data through BespoxAI?

Access is controlled at two levels. Within BespoxAI, only the users you invite to your tenant can ask questions of your environment. Beneath that, the read-only credential you provision defines what any query can ever reach — and you can scope it to specific tables and companies, audit it, or revoke it entirely at any time.

Is our data stored, or used to train AI models?

Query results are returned to answer your question and are not stored by BespoxAI. Your environment data is isolated per tenant and is not used to train AI models — ours or anyone else's.

What AI powers BespoxAI?

BespoxAI is currently powered by Anthropic's Claude models, grounded in the object index of your specific environment. We name our model provider openly because you deserve to know what's answering questions about your business — no black boxes.

Commercial
We own our licenses outright. Does this put us back on a subscription treadmill?

Honest answer: BespoxAI is a subscription — but it subscribes you to the thing NAV never had (intelligence), not the thing you already own (your ERP). Your perpetual license stays exactly that: yours, in perpetuity. Cancel BespoxAI tomorrow and your system runs precisely as it does today.

What happens if we migrate to Business Central someday?

Nothing is wasted. The same platform serves Business Central, and the indexed knowledge of your customisations makes an eventual migration easier, not stranded — you'd be arriving with a complete, searchable map of everything your system does.

How is BespoxAI priced?

By product line, with transparent pricing published on our pricing section. You can start free — no credit card, no commitment.

How does payment work for development?

The principle is simple: AI involvement is covered by your subscription. Human involvement is quoted. Feasibility assessments and AI-guided requirements building are included in your plan — explore every idea at no extra cost. When you're ready for a senior developer to evaluate your specification, that review is a paid step, quoted before it happens.

Development itself is fixed-price, quoted from your locked specification, with a deposit to commence and the balance on delivery. Two invoices. No timesheets, no progress claims, no payment chasing — the overhead of billing complexity is engineered out of the process, not baked into your price.
