About BespoxAI

Someone has to serve the ERP
Microsoft left behind.

The thesis

Hundreds of thousands of businesses still run their operations on Dynamics NAV. Microsoft's roadmap moved on; their businesses didn't stop. The partner channel is consolidating around cloud migrations, C/AL developers are redirecting to AL work faster than they're being replaced, and the customers who made one of the most financially sensible licensing decisions in ERP history — perpetual ownership — are being told the only path forward is to abandon it.

We think that's wrong. Your system isn't a problem to be migrated away. It's an asset to be made smarter.

BespoxAI exists to give NAV and Business Central customers the modern intelligence layer their platform never received — grounded in their actual environment, on their terms, with no migration as the price of admission.

Who we are

BespoxAI was founded in New Zealand by developers who have spent more than two decades inside NAV — writing C/AL across dealer management, distribution and manufacturing environments, and supporting real customers on everything from NAV 2009 to Business Central. We've debugged posting routines at 2am before month-end. We've untangled dimension corrections, rebuilt broken cost adjustments, and kept twenty-year-old customisations running through version upgrades their original authors never imagined.

That matters, because the hardest part of serving this market isn't the AI. It's knowing what a NAV system actually is — how the object model fits together, why the customisations exist, and what can and cannot be safely touched.

Why we built it

BespoxAI was built by Navision developers, not vibe coders. It wasn't prompted into existence by people who discovered ERP last year. Every design decision in the platform — the per-environment object index, the grounding of every answer in your actual system, the read-only-by-design connectivity — comes from people who know what an object index is, because we've spent our careers inside one.

AI that guesses about standard NAV is worse than useless in a customised environment — it's confidently wrong. So we built the opposite: intelligence that starts from your tables, your codeunits, your bespoke ranges, and reasons from there.
